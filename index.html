Appointment Communication Consent

To help coordinate your medical care, our scheduling partner Cybersenz Inc may contact you regarding your appointment request.

By providing your phone number and submitting the request form, you agree to receive:

  • Appointment scheduling calls
  • Appointment confirmations
  • Appointment reminders
  • An automated voice call from our scheduling assistant

Consent is not a condition of treatment. Message frequency varies. Message & data rates may apply.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or call 510-582-8555.

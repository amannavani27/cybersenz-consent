Patient Communication Consent

Advanced Pain Management and Rehab Medical Group uses Cybersenz Inc as a scheduling communication service provider.

How patients opt in

Patients request an appointment through the clinic website or through staff intake. During submission they provide their phone number and check a consent checkbox agreeing to receive:

  • Appointment scheduling calls
  • Appointment confirmation messages
  • Appointment reminders

Submission of the form provides express consent to receive SMS and automated voice calls. Consent is not a condition of treatment.

Message details

  • Transactional only
  • No marketing
  • Message frequency varies
  • Message and data rates may apply

Opt-Out

Patients may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
